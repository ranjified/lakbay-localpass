# Lakbay LocalPass User Journeys

## Tourist

The tourist uses My Lakbay Pass to select a trip style, discover places, scan QR codes, order local food, request a vehicle, book a stay, request a guide, and track rewards.

Main flows:

1. Order food through Taste Trail
2. Book a stay through Stay Match
3. Request vehicle through Sakay Tayabas
4. Request guide through Kwentong Gabay
5. Scan QR and earn points
6. Save coupons and badges

## Merchant

The merchant uses Local Seller Counter to manage products, accept food and pasalubong orders, prepare pickup requests, create coupons, and attach offers to tourism routes.

Unique feature: Tourism Boost Card.

## Accommodation Owner

The accommodation owner uses Stay Tayabas Desk to manage room listings, booking requests, amenities, check-in instructions, and local add-ons.

Unique feature: Stay Match tags rooms by tourism purpose.

## Transport Provider

The transport provider uses Sakay Tayabas Board to accept ride requests, publish vehicle packages, view pickup details, and complete tourist-friendly route loops.

Unique feature: Route Buddy.

## Tour Guide

The guide uses Kwentong Gabay Desk to publish packages, accept tour requests, and use story cards during tours.

Unique feature: Story Cards.

## Event Organizer

The event organizer uses Tayabas Events Desk to publish events and connect them to food, rides, stays, and QR challenges.

Unique feature: Event Trip Builder.

## Tourism Staff

Tourism staff uses Tourism Command Center to manage official destinations, QR content, approvals, routes, events, and analytics.

Unique feature: Tourism Heat Pulse.

## Admin

Admin uses Platform Control Room to manage users, roles, reports, platform settings, and trust queue items.

Unique feature: Trust and Safety Queue.
