# Validation Summary

This file tracks product validation for Lakbay LocalPass.

## Tourist Pain Points

- Tourism information is scattered across social media, search, and word of mouth.
- Visitors often miss heritage stories and smaller local businesses.
- Trip planning, food pickup, rides, stays, and guide inquiries are disconnected.

## Merchant Feedback

- Target: 10 local businesses including restaurants, cafes, carinderias, and pasalubong sellers.
- Questions: Would route-linked visibility, preorder inquiries, and LocalPass coupons help bring visitors to your business?

## Tourism Office Insights

- Target: City Tourism Office feedback.
- Questions: Which destinations need better storytelling, QR signage, visitor analytics, and content controls?

## Prototype Test Findings

Suggested tasks:

1. Find a heritage destination.
2. Save a destination.
3. Scan a demo QR.
4. Unlock a story.
5. Submit a food or pasalubong inquiry.
6. Check LocalPass points.
7. View request status.
8. Claim a coupon.

## Most Useful Features

- Destination discovery and save-to-trip.
- QR heritage story unlocks.
- LocalPass points, badges, and coupons.
- Simple local service inquiry forms.

## Concerns Raised By Users

- Whether demo inquiries are real bookings.
- Whether QR scanning tracks exact location.
- Whether local partners can respond quickly.

## Changes Made After Feedback

- Request form copy states that MVP inquiries are demo-only.
- Scan copy states that QR scanning is for stories and demo check-ins.
- Manual QR entry is available even without camera scanning.

## Validation Targets

- 10 tourists or visitors.
- 10 local businesses.
- 5 transport providers or tour guides.
- 3 accommodation owners.
- Tourism staff feedback.
